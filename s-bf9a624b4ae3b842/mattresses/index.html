Alberta King mattresses

Top 10 for Mike and Sasha, best quality first. Every pick is made in exactly 96x96. Checked Oct 25, 2026.

Size: the designer's frame is a custom low Alberta King, exactly 96x96 in, already ordered (Telegram, Sep 21 and Oct 16). It's a real but rare size, also sold as Vermont King, Alaskan King Mini and Bel-Air King. No mainstream brand makes it (not Tempur-Pedic, Saatva, Stearns & Foster, Purple, Sleep Number, Naturepedic or Avocado), so every pick below is a custom or specialist build.

My pick: Vispring Bedstead Superb, as two zipped 48x96 halves, each side its own tension. The best-made mattress that truly sits in a low frame without its own base, natural fillings that sleep cool, a 30-year guarantee, and zipped halves so you don't feel each other move. No custom size can be exchanged, so lie on the tensions first at Bedroom & More (1676 Market St, SF); McRoskey (#2) is 10 minutes away on Alameda St, so both fit in one afternoon.

Firmness for Mike's neck: the designer's three picks are all soft. The evidence favors medium to medium-firm (about 5-7 of 10): a review of 39 studies found "a medium-firm mattress promotes comfort, sleep quality and rachis alignment" (Caggiari 2021), and nothing supports soft for pain. The neck is mostly the pillow: its height has to match how far the shoulder sinks. If Sasha wants softer, a bed with a different firmness per side solves it.

For the low frame: every pick except Hästens works on a platform with no box spring. Ask the designer that the base is either solid with vents or slats 3 in apart or less, with center legs across the 96 in.

Custom means final sale almost everywhere. Only FloBeds (20 years of layer swaps), Custom Comfort (365-night rebuild) and Savvy Rest (90-day layer exchange) let you fix the firmness afterward.

Sheets: 96x96 fitted sheets from Ocochi ($290 organic cotton, 16 in pocket) or Alaskan King Bed Co. ($375); take 18 in pockets for the 15-16 in beds.

My #1

Vispring Bedstead Superb, two zipped 48x96 halves. It's the only top-tier handmade mattress that confirms exactly 96x96 and is built to sit in a frame like the designer's with no base of its own. Each of you picks a spring tension, so Mike gets medium-firm support for his neck and Sasha can go softer, and the zip keeps your movements apart. Natural wool and horsehair sleep cool and the springs carry a 30-year guarantee. If you'd rather keep the option to re-tune after delivery, take FloBeds (#3) instead.
