Toys for Mark

20 picks for brain development, ordered by how much each adds for him (2 years 4 months, Montessori class, Russian and English at home). Prices checked live on Oct 25, 2026.

If you buy only three – $159.97

  • Magna-Tiles Classic 32-Piece Set – $49.99
    Builds for years, and spatial building is the play most tied to later math.
  • Toniebox Lite My Story Set (with a Creative-Tonie) – $79.99
    Nothing else he has adds Russian; family voices on demand, no screen.
  • HABA First Orchard – $29.99
    His first rules game, made for his exact age: waiting, turns, losing together.

Already has (Amazon orders): FanttikRide N7 and ANPABO electric wiggle cars, Fisher-Price Laugh & Learn DJ Table. Nothing below repeats them.

"3+ label" is almost always a small-parts warning, not a thinking level. Fine with a grown-up there; put the small pieces away after.

Tip from Montessori: 6–8 toys on a low shelf, swap a few each week. Fewer at once gets deeper play.

    Magna-Tiles Classic 32-Piece Set

    Toniebox Lite My Story Set (with a Creative-Tonie)

    Language · Russian · ages 1+ fits now

    $79.99 · tonies.com

    His class is in English, so Russian needs help. Grandparents record stories and songs into the tonies app from their phones; he plays them by putting the figure on the box, no screen.
